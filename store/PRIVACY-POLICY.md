# Dice Rollar Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-06-10**

Dice Rollar is a local-only Android app. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Roll history is stored only on your device using local storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app. The app does not connect to the internet, contains no advertisements, includes no third-party analytics, and requests no permissions.
