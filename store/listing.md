## Full description
<!-- length: 900 / 4000 -->
Dice Rollar is a fast, no-nonsense dice roller for your phone. Whether you're at a tabletop RPG night, taking a study break, or just can't decide between two options, it gets you to a roll in a single tap. Built local-first, no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

**Features**

- 7 dice types: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100
- Multi-die roller — roll any combination at once (e.g. 4d6, 2d20+5)
- Modifier stepper for quick +/- adjustments
- Full roll history so you never lose a result
- Dark theme designed for low-light game tables
- No ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs
- Tiny footprint, around 3 MB installed
- Fully offline-first — no internet needed, ever

**Made for**

Tabletop RPG players (D&D, Pathfinder), board game nights, teachers running classroom activities, and anyone who needs a quick, fair, random number to break a tie.

**No accounts, no ads, no nonsense.**
