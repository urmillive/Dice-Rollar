Dice Rollar is a clean, fast dice roller built for Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, and any tabletop RPG that needs honest random rolls.

Every roll is animated — you see the dice tumble and settle on a real face, not just a number popping out of nowhere. It looks like dice. It feels like dice.

WHAT YOU GET
• Every standard polyhedral die: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100
• Roll up to 10 dice at once
• Add a modifier from −99 to +99 (perfect for attack rolls, skill checks, damage)
• Standard dice notation in every result (2d20+5, 3d6, 1d8−2)
• Roll history kept on your device — see your last 20 rolls at a glance
• Clear history any time with one tap
• Each die shown as its real shape: triangle for d4, hexagon for d20, and so on

WHAT YOU DON'T GET
• No ads
• No signup, no account, no email harvesting
• No tracking, no analytics
• No internet permission — works fully offline
• No bloat, no in-app currency, no nag screens

WHY ANOTHER DICE ROLLER?
Most dice apps either look like a spreadsheet or stuff you with ads and "premium tiers" for features that should be free. Dice Rollar is the opposite — small, fast, native, and free, with the dice front and center where they belong.

Built with Jetpack Compose for smooth animation on every modern Android device.

PERFECT FOR
• D&D 5e, Pathfinder 2e, Call of Cthulhu, Shadowrun, GURPS — anything that uses polyhedral dice
• Game masters running encounters
• Players who left their physical dice at home
• Anyone who needs a quick honest random number
