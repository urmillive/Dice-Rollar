# Privacy Policy — Dice Rollar

**Effective date:** 2026-06-11
**Last updated:** 2026-06-11

## Summary

Dice Rollar does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works fully offline. Everything stays on your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Dice Rollar does not collect any information about you, your device, or how you use the app. There is no signup, no account, no email, no telemetry.

## What we store

Your roll history (the last 20 rolls) is stored locally on your device using Android's standard DataStore. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by tapping the clear-history button inside the app, or by clearing the app's data in Android Settings.

## What we share

**Nothing.** Because we do not collect data, there is nothing to share. Dice Rollar does not contact any server, does not send analytics, and does not include any third-party advertising SDKs.

## Permissions

Dice Rollar requests no special Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, files, or network.

## Children

Dice Rollar is suitable for ages 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Because the app collects no information at all, there is nothing to delete on request — uninstalling the app removes all local data.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Dice Rollar adds any data collection (for example, opt-in crash reporting or anonymous analytics), this policy will be updated and the change announced in the app's release notes before the new version ships.
